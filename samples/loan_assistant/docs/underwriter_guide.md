# Underwriter guide: referred pre-approvals

## What you receive
Applications with a repayment score between 0.40 and 0.70 are referred to you with the
score and the customer's answers.

## How to read the score
The score is the model's estimate of the probability of repayment over 12 months. It is
calibrated, so 0.55 means roughly 55 in 100 similar applicants repaid. It does not take
into account anything the customer did not tell the assistant.

## Your decision
You can approve or decline any referred application. Record a reason for every decision;
it is logged with your name. If the score looks wrong, escalate to the Model Owner.

## Training
All underwriters complete a one-hour session on the model and its limits before they
receive referrals.
