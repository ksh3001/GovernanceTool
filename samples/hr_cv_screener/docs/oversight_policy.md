# Human oversight policy: CV Screener

## Principle
Hiring decisions affect people's lives. Automated scores support the recruitment
team and do not replace their judgement.

## Rules
1. A human recruiter reviews every rejection before the candidate is notified.
2. Recruiters can overrule any score and move a candidate to the shortlist.
3. Recruiters receive a short training on how to read the tool's output.

## Responsibilities
The Head of Talent owns this policy and reviews it once a year.
